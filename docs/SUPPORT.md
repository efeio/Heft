# Support for Heft

Welcome to the **Heft** support page. We are here to help you get the most out of your weight tracking experience.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Does Heft share my data with anyone?
No. Heft does not use any cloud servers or third-party tracking services. All your weight entries and profile information remain strictly on your device.

### 2. How does Apple Health synchronization work?
Heft integrates directly with Apple Health:
- When enabled, your logged weigh-ins are written to Apple Health automatically.
- You can also import past weight records from Apple Health in the **Settings** screen.
- You can turn Apple Health sync on or off at any time.

### 3. Can I change units between kg and lbs?
Yes. Go to **Settings > UNITS** to toggle your preferred weight unit (`kg` or `lbs`) and height unit (`cm` or `ft / in`). Your existing data will automatically display in your selected unit without losing precision.

### 4. How do I delete or edit a logged weigh-in?
In the **History** tab:
- **Edit:** Tap on any entry row to adjust the weight or date.
- **Delete:** Swipe left on any entry row and tap "Delete", or open the entry and tap "Delete This Entry".
